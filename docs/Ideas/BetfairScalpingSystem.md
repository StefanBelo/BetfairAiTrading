# Betfair Scalping System — Strategy Overview

**Source:** [Betfair Scalping System — The UK Betting Forum](https://www.theukbettingforum.co.uk/XenForo/threads/betfair-scalping-system.71160/)

## What is Betfair Scalping?
Betfair scalping is a trading strategy on the Betfair Exchange that aims to make small, quick profits by exploiting minor price movements in highly liquid markets, typically just before a horse race starts. The approach is to back (bet for) or lay (bet against) a selection and then quickly close the position for a small profit, ideally before the market moves against you.

## Key Points from the Forum Discussion
- **Market Selection:**
  - Focus on large, liquid markets (e.g., UK horse racing pre-race markets).
  - Races with two strong favourites are ideal, as their prices are most reactive to each other.
  - Avoid illiquid or volatile markets.

- **Timing:**
  - Most volatility and trading opportunities occur in the last 5 minutes before the off, especially the final 2 minutes.
  - Trades should be short-lived (ideally in the market for less than a minute).

- **Core Concepts:**
  - **Support & Resistance:**
    - Identify price levels where odds repeatedly bounce (support = price floor, resistance = price ceiling).
    - Back near support, lay near resistance, aiming to profit from the bounce.
    - Watch for breakouts, where price moves beyond these levels.
  - **Crossover Points:**
    - Key odds (2, 3, 4, 6, 10, etc.) have different tick sizes; laying at these points can be advantageous due to asymmetric risk/reward.
  - **Weight of Money:**
    - Observe the amounts waiting to be matched on both back and lay sides.
    - Large imbalances can signal likely price movement, but beware of market manipulation.

- **Execution:**
  - Fast software and a stable internet connection are essential.
  - Practice with small stakes and avoid greed.
  - Always have an exit plan (stop loss) and stick to it.
  - Protect your bank; patience and discipline are critical.

- **Psychology & Risk:**
  - Scalping is stressful and requires quick reactions.
  - Most traders lose money; only a small percentage are consistently profitable.
  - Avoid letting trades go in-play; this is considered poor risk management.

## Example Strategy (from the thread)
1. **Find a race with two clear favourites.**
2. **Monitor their price ranges (support/resistance) on the ladder.**
3. **Wait for one favourite to hit the bottom of its range and the other the top.**
4. **Back the favourite at support or lay the second favourite at resistance.**
5. **Exit for a small profit as the prices bounce, or cut the loss if the price breaks out.**

## Summary
Betfair scalping is a technical, high-frequency trading approach that relies on understanding market microstructure, price resistance/support, and rapid execution. It is not easy, requires significant practice, and carries real risks, but can be profitable for disciplined, patient traders.
