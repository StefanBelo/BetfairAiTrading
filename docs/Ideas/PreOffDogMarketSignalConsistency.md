# Strategy Idea: Consistency of Pre-Off Dog Market Signals

## Summary
This discussion explores the reliability and consistency of a betting signal derived from pre-off dog (greyhound) markets, based on backtesting results from 2025, 2024, and 2023. The main concern is the lack of consistent performance across years, raising questions about overfitting, market evolution, and the validity of the signal.

## Main Topics & Insights

### 1. **Backtest Consistency Across Years**
- 2025 data shows promising results.
- 2024 and 2023 data show very different (worse) results.
- Consistency across years is expected for a robust signal.

### 2. **Market Evolution**
- Markets change over time (e.g., declining volumes).
- A signal that works in one year but not others may be due to market evolution or structural changes.

### 3. **Overfitting Concerns**
- Good results in 2025 may be due to overfitting to recent data.
- Overfitting leads to poor generalization to other years.

### 4. **Signal Validity and Risk**
- A signal with unexplained historical underperformance is risky to trade live.
- Need to understand and explain discrepancies before trusting the signal.
- Drawdowns are inevitable; confidence in the signal's validity is crucial.

### 5. **Possible Explanations for Discrepancy**
- The edge is real but due to a recent market participant or change (may disappear).
- The edge is real but has long drawdowns (may be untradeable alone).
- The edge is not real; results are spurious.

### 6. **Volume Effects**
- Some have observed signals getting stronger as market volumes decline.
- Lower liquidity can change market dynamics and signal effectiveness.

## Actionable Ideas
- Investigate reasons for year-to-year discrepancies.
- Test for overfitting (e.g., cross-validation, out-of-sample testing).
- Analyze market structure changes (volumes, participants).
- Consider ensemble approaches if signal is unstable alone.
- Only deploy live if confident in the signal's robustness and explainability.
