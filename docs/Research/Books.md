# Recommended Books and Papers on Betting, Trading, and Decision Science

## Betting Markets & Value

- [The Logic of Sports Betting](https://www.amazon.co.uk/Logic-Sports-Betting-Ed-Miller-ebook/dp/B07RG6H8C2)  
	US-centric but an excellent introduction to understanding betting markets and how to think about value.
- [Trading Bases: A Story About Wall Street, Gambling, and Baseball](https://www.amazon.co.uk/Trading-Bases-Fortune-Betting-Baseball/dp/0451415175)
- [Statistical Sports Models in Excel](https://www.amazon.co.uk/Statistical-Sports-Models-Excel-Andrew/dp/1079013458)
- [Precision: Statistical and Mathematical Methods in Horse Racing](https://www.amazon.co.uk/Precision-Statistical-Mathematical-Methods-Racing/dp/1432768522)
- [Efficiency of Racetrack Betting Markets](https://www.amazon.co.uk/Efficiency-Racetrack-Scientific-Financial-Economics/dp/981320351X)
- [Kelly Capital Growth Investment Criterion](https://www.amazon.co.uk/Kelly-Capital-Growth-Investment-Criterion/dp/9814383139)
- [Retail Options Trading](https://www.amazon.co.uk/Retail-Options-Trading-Andrew-Mack-ebook/dp/B0DL7FCKKC)

## Football (Soccer) Modelling

- [Dixon-Coles Model: Seminal Paper for Modelling Football (PDF)](https://betfairlightweight.slack.com/files/UBS7QANF3/F010NTJJK88/dixoncoles.pdf?origin_team=T4G9NBD2M&origin_channel=C4HL6EZTQ)
- [Dixon 1998: In-Running Football (PDF)](https://betfairlightweight.slack.com/files/UBS7QANF3/F010A4MK9MY/dixon1998.pdf?origin_team=T4G9NBD2M&origin_channel=C4HL6EZTQ)

## Horse Racing

- [A Classic Horse Racing Paper: Benter 1994 (PDF)](https://www.gwern.net/docs/statistics/decision/1994-benter.pdf)

## Tennis Modelling

- [An Excellent Starting Point for Tennis Modelling (PhD Thesis)](http://www.strategicgames.com.au/PhD.pdf)
- [Chasing Points: A Season on the Pro Tennis Circuit](https://www.amazon.co.uk/Chasing-Points-Season-Tennis-Circuit-ebook/dp/B08HB5Y64R)  
	A romantic portrayal of life on the edges of the Challenger tennis tour.

## Probabilistic Forecasting & Decision Science

- [Thinking, Fast and Slow](https://www.amazon.co.uk/Thinking-Fast-Slow-Daniel-Kahneman-ebook/dp/B005MJFA2W)  
	Outstanding book on cognitive biases.
- [The Signal and the Noise: The Art and Science of Prediction](https://www.amazon.co.uk/Signal-Noise-Art-Science-Prediction-ebook/dp/B0097JYVAU)  
	Excellent introduction to probabilistic forecasting in general.

## Trading & Memoirs

- [Man for All Markets: Beating the Odds](https://www.amazon.co.uk/Man-All-Markets-Beating-Street-ebook/dp/B07ZWJFYW5)  
	Inspiration for how far you can go.
- [Damsel in Distressed: Golden Age of Hedge Funds](https://www.amazon.co.uk/Damsel-Distressed-Golden-Hedge-Funds-ebook/dp/B0988B7HSC)  
	One of the more obscure trading memoirs but thoroughly enjoyable.

## Wild Card / Startups

- [Burn Rate: Launching a Startup and Losing My Mind](https://www.amazon.co.uk/Burn-Rate-Launching-Startup-Losing-ebook/dp/B09CCP2VWX)
