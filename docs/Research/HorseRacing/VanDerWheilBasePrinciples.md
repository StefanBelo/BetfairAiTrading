# Van der Wheil (VDW) – Base Principles

## Core idea

VDW’s method is a structured way to identify the most reliable horse in a race by combining class, consistency, ability, form, capability, and probability into a single judgement.

---

## Key components

### 1. Class and ability

- **Class:**  
  The overall level of races a horse has been contesting, judged mainly by race type and prize money.

- **Ability:**  
  A horse’s proven performance at a given class, often proxied by total prize money won and the class of races in which that money was earned.

### 2. Consistency

- **Recent placings:**  
  Use the horse’s last three completed runs to build a picture of reliability (finishing positions, not just wins).

- **Numerical picture:**  
  Consistency is treated as a numerical summary of those placings, used to compare runners and highlight the most reliable.

### 3. Form

- **True vs false form:**  
  Decide whether recent runs genuinely reflect the horse’s current ability (true form) or are misleading due to circumstances (false form).

- **Context of runs:**  
  Consider going, distance, pace, and race shape when judging whether a run is a valid indicator of present form.

### 4. Capability

- **Conditions today:**  
  Check that the horse is demonstrably capable under the specific conditions of the current race:
  - distance  
  - going  
  - weight carried  
  - class level

- **Proven suitability:**  
  Preference is given to horses that have already performed well under similar conditions.

### 5. Probability (market)

- **Betting forecast:**  
  Use the pre-race forecast as an expression of market probability; most winners come from the first few in the betting.

- **Shortlist from the market:**  
  Focus attention on horses that are both strong on class/consistency and prominent in the forecast.

---

## Erin numbers (high-level only)

- **Purpose:**  
  Erin numbers are a consistency-related tool used in VDW’s Erin Foods Hurdle examples to highlight “probable” winners.

- **Status:**  
  The exact formula is not fully specified; they are treated as an auxiliary numerical view of consistency rather than a standalone mechanical system.

---

## Working rule of thumb

> Constant Form + Ability + Capability + Probability + Hard Work = Winners

In practice, this means:

1. **Identify the class/ability horse.**  
2. **Check its consistency and form.**  
3. **Confirm capability under today’s conditions.**  
4. **Cross-check with the betting forecast (probability).**  
5. **Make a final, non-mechanical judgement based on the combined picture.**
