# Horse Racing Data, Strategy, and AI Harnessing

I would like to discuss the following topics:
*   Horse racing data and available online sources.
*   Data processing and analysis techniques.
*   Building strategies or ratings using publicly available data.
*   Utilizing an "AI Harness" for data analysis, automation, and strategy execution.

There are several ways you can participate in this challenge:

The primary dataset used is the Timeform data published by Betfair on their website. This data is freely available; those interested can copy and paste it into Excel to develop their own ratings. Currently, Betfair does not block the parsing of this specific data, allowing you to automate the collection process yourself.

I can provide daily data for one market in JSON format. While I recognize that the number of features may be limited for advanced machine learning applications, this challenge is primarily aimed at those who are just beginning their journey into data processing and various forms of analysis.

For those interested in automation, I am happy to share my own application for free (compatible with Windows) for those who wish to set up an "agentic" support system.

I especially welcome anyone with experience in AI, web-based Conversed AI, chatbots, or specialized AI tools—many of whom may be familiar with the concept of an "AI Harness."

My current approach utilizes Betfair's Timeform data combined with the following prompt:
